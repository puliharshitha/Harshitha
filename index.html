02 / A little about me

A curious mind with a soft spot for big ideas and tiny details.

My background in computer science helps me connect design decisions to how products actually work. I love the space between logic and imagination: asking better questions, finding the simplest useful answer, and collaborating closely with developers to bring it to life.

My design approach is collaborative, iterative, and grounded in real user needs — with enough personality to make a product feel memorable.

03 / How I get there

Good design is
a team sport.

A flexible process, not a rigid checklist. I adapt the tools to the problem, keep feedback loops short, and make decisions visible.

STEP 01🔎

Discover

Understand people, context, constraints, and what success should look like.

STEP 02🧩

Define

Turn what we learn into a clear problem, priorities, and a shared direction.

STEP 03✏️

Design

Explore ideas, map flows, prototype, and refine the details that matter.

STEP 04🪄

Validate

Test with people, learn from feedback, and collaborate through implementation.

“The best interfaces don’t ask for attention.
They make everything else feel easier.”
A principle I bring to every project ✳
